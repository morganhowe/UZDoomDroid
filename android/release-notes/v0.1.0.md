## UZDoomDroid 0.1.0

The controller-only Android port of [UZDoom](https://github.com/UZDoom/UZDoom), now with Vulkan and dual-screen support.
Based on UZDoom commit [`678ed12`](https://github.com/UZDoom/UZDoom/commit/678ed12027fec6257a08bfcdbabba06bfcebf8de).

Installs over 0.0.3 and keeps your games, saves and settings.

### New

- **Vulkan renderer.** UZDoom's full-featured backend, for demanding mods. It's used automatically where the GPU
  supports it; other devices fall back to OpenGL ES. If Vulkan ever crashes on start-up, the next launch switches to
  OpenGL ES by itself. To choose, use *Options → Display Options → Video Mode → Preferred rendering API* and restart.
- **Dual screens.** On handhelds with a second screen (e.g. AYN Thor), the game plays on the main screen and the
  second screen shows a live map that follows you, with kills, secrets, items, time and battery. *Options → Automap
  Options* has *Map on second screen* and *Second screen map zoom*.
- **Extra buttons can be bound**, such as the additional shoulder buttons on Retroid handhelds. They appear as
  *Joy1*–*Joy20* in *Options → Customize Controls*.
- **Battery level on the automap** (top right). *Options → Automap Options → Show battery level* turns it off.
- **New app icon.**

### Changes

- **Sharp pixels at full resolution.** The game renders at the screen's own resolution, with unfiltered textures, by
  default. Your texture filter and resolution scale settings are reset to these defaults once.
- **Controller-friendly control setup.** In *Customize Controls*, **A** changes a binding, **X** clears it and
  **START** cancels.

### Requirements

Android 8.0+, 64-bit ARM, a game controller. A Vulkan-capable GPU is recommended (OpenGL ES 2.0+ otherwise).
**No game data included.** Bring your own IWAD (e.g. `doom2.wad`) or use the free [Freedoom](https://freedoom.github.io/).

### Known limitations

- VP8/VP9 cutscene videos don't play.
- Tested on two devices (a Retroid Pocket Nova and an AYN Thor).
