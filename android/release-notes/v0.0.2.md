## UZDoomDroid 0.0.2

A bug-fix test build of the controller-only Android port of [UZDoom](https://github.com/UZDoom/UZDoom).
Based on UZDoom commit [`678ed12`](https://github.com/UZDoom/UZDoom/commit/678ed12027fec6257a08bfcdbabba06bfcebf8de).

### ⚠️ Uninstall 0.0.1 first

Each build is signed with a different temporary key, so Android won't install this over 0.0.1.
**Uninstalling deletes your imported game files, saves and settings.** In 0.0.1 and 0.0.2 the saves and settings are
in hidden folders that Android doesn't show over USB, so they can't be backed up. Copy your `.wad` files out of
`Android/data/io.github.morganhowe.uzdoomdroid/files/wads/` first, or re-import them with **Y** afterwards.

### Fixes

- **Secret chime and other sounds now play.** FLAC, Ogg Vorbis, Opus and MP3 audio was silent because the decoding
  libraries weren't included. This affected the "secret found" chime, about 20 other built-in sounds (ice deaths,
  sparks, earthquakes, railgun) and OGG/MP3 music in mods. The app now includes libsndfile (with FLAC, Ogg, Vorbis
  and Opus) and mpg123.
- **Menus no longer scroll by themselves.** Pressing the D-pad while a controller was connecting or waking up could
  leave a key "held", and the menu would scroll forever.

### Requirements

Android 8.0+, 64-bit ARM, OpenGL ES 2.0+ (3.x recommended), a game controller.
**No game data included.** Bring your own IWAD (e.g. `doom2.wad`) or use the free [Freedoom](https://freedoom.github.io/).

### Known limitations

- Vulkan is disabled. VP8/VP9 cutscene videos don't play.
- Tested on one device only.
- Signed with a temporary build key (see above).
