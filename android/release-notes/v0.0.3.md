## UZDoomDroid 0.0.3

A bug-fix test build of the controller-only Android port of [UZDoom](https://github.com/UZDoom/UZDoom).
Based on UZDoom commit [`678ed12`](https://github.com/UZDoom/UZDoom/commit/678ed12027fec6257a08bfcdbabba06bfcebf8de).

### ⚠️ Uninstall 0.0.1 / 0.0.2 first (for the last time)

This is the first release signed with a **permanent key**. From now on, new versions install over this one and keep your
saves. Older builds used temporary keys, so Android won't install this over them: uninstall first.
Uninstalling deletes your games, saves and settings. In 0.0.1 and 0.0.2 the saves are in hidden folders and can't be
backed up, but you can copy your `.wad` files out of `Android/data/io.github.morganhowe.uzdoomdroid/files/wads/`
first, or re-import them with **Y** afterwards.

### Fixes

- **Menus no longer scroll by themselves.** Android's tilt sensor was being treated as a joystick, so holding the
  device at an angle scrolled menus down. It's now ignored. Pressing the D-pad while a controller connects can no
  longer leave a key stuck either.
- **Sleep and Home pause the game.** Sound stops immediately. When you come back, the main menu is open and the game
  doesn't fast-forward through the time you were away.
- **No grey bar at the top** after sleep or returning from the home screen.
- **Saves, screenshots and settings can be backed up.** They're now in visible folders in
  `Android/data/io.github.morganhowe.uzdoomdroid/files/`: `savegames/`, `screenshots/` and `config/uzdoom/`.
- **The launcher layout survives importing files.** Status messages could squeeze the game and mod lists until the
  app was restarted.

Also since 0.0.1: FLAC/Ogg/Opus/MP3 sounds and music play (including the secret-found chime).

### Requirements

Android 8.0+, 64-bit ARM, OpenGL ES 2.0+ (3.x recommended), a game controller.
**No game data included.** Bring your own IWAD (e.g. `doom2.wad`) or use the free [Freedoom](https://freedoom.github.io/).

### Known limitations

- Vulkan is disabled. VP8/VP9 cutscene videos don't play.
- Tested on one device only.
